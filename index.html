About you

Type the name of your hall or hostel. If you live in a hall, say whether you are in the Main hall or the Annex, for example "Akuafo Hall, Main" or "Akuafo Hall, Annex". If you stay off campus, type your hostel or area, for example "Off campus, East Legon". Campus is for your records only.

My three semester goals

Write each goal, then break it into small steps. Tick a step when you finish it and your progress bar moves. Your progress is saved on this device and appears in your PDF and Excel downloads.

My weekly reflection

At the end of each week, complete your reflection below and include it in your feedback if you want your advisor to review it.

Choose one day each week to review your plan.

Saving keeps this week in your diary on this device. It is not sent to your advisor until you tick it in the feedback section.

My reflection diary

My monthly summary

At the end of the month, build a summary from your weekly entries, then edit it into a short summary of what went well, what was hard and what you will change.

Weekly timetable

Create your weekly plan once and reuse it each week. Add lectures, tutorials, study sessions, meals, commuting, rest, clubs and spiritual activities. Update the week beginning date and edit entries when your classes or commitments change; you do not need to rebuild the timetable every week.

Sleep, meals, commuting, rest, church, devotion and personal development are planning reminders. They are not forms to complete each day, and you can leave out any you prefer.

Tap a box to choose an activity, or pick Other to type your own. Change a time slot's start or end at the left, or tap ✕ to delete it. Use "Show hours" to see fewer rows. On a phone, scroll sideways to see every day.
