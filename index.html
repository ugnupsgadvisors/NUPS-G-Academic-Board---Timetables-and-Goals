Student Personal Timetable

Plan your classes, study, rest, goals and routines—one week at a time.

Designed by the NUPSG Academic Board

1. About you

Your entries auto-save in this browser on this device. Press “Save on this device” before leaving, and avoid shared/public devices for private goals.

2. Weekly timetable

Create your weekly plan once and reuse it each week. Add lectures, tutorials, study sessions, meals, commute, rest, clubs and spiritual activities. Update the week beginning date and edit entries when your classes or commitments change; you do not need to rebuild the timetable every week.

On mobile, your entries are kept when you switch days. Copy a day to reuse its schedule, then edit individual entries.

3. My three semester goals

Make each goal specific and realistic. For example: “Study Biology for 45 minutes after lectures on four days each week.”

Only include what you are comfortable sharing with your advisor.

4. Daily routines & weekly check-in

Daily routines are reminders for your usual week, not a checklist you must fill in every day. Set them once and change them when needed. At the end of each week, complete your reflection below and include it in your weekly Google Form submission if you want your advisor to review it.

Choose one day each week to review your plan. This website does not send automatic notifications; set a weekly reminder on your phone or calendar.

This reflection is included in the submission summary you send through Google Forms.

5. Save, download or send to your advisor

Save stores your draft only in this browser on this device. PDF prints the full planner. Excel downloads an editable CSV spreadsheet that opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Submit your timetable and goals once at the start of the semester or when your plan changes. For your weekly check-in, update the reflection, prepare a fresh summary, and submit it through the advisor Google Form. The timetable is saved in this browser, so it can be reused on the same device.

For the weekly check-in, copy the summary, open the Google Form, and paste it into the form’s “Complete timetable summary” field before submitting. Update your reflection first. Your advisor sees only what you submit through the form.
